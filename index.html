Thakurani Foundation · Mumbai, India

Every Life Deserves Warmth & Dignity

Thakurani Foundation shelters orphaned children, cares for the elderly, and feeds the hungry — one compassionate act at a time, in the heart of Mumbai.

Learn Our Story

We believe every human being — young or old, forgotten or unseen — deserves a safe home, a warm meal, and a community that cares. Thakurani Foundation was born from that belief, and it guides everything we do.

Rooted in compassion. Growing through community.

What We Do

Every day, we channel care and resources where they are needed most across three core pillars.

500+ Children Sheltered

Orphanage Care

We provide a safe, nurturing home for children without families — offering education, nutrition, emotional support, and the warmth of belonging.

300+ Elders Cared For

Elderly Support

Our elders deserve dignity and companionship. We offer daily care, medical support, and a loving community for senior citizens who have no one to turn to.

10,000+ Meals Served

Food & Shelter

No one should go to bed hungry or without a roof. We run daily meal programmes and emergency shelter for the most vulnerable in Mumbai.

A Story Born from Compassion

Thakurani Foundation began with a single act of kindness — a meal shared with a child on the streets of Mumbai. That moment grew into a movement.

Over fifteen years, we have built homes, fed thousands, and held the hands of the elderly in their final years. Every life we touch reminds us why we began.

We are not a large institution. We are a family — volunteers, donors, and the people we serve — all woven together by the belief that no one should be left behind.

— The Thakurani Foundation Family
